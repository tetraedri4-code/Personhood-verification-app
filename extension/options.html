Personhood badges

The public list of verified accounts and like counts. Downloaded whole; the directory is never asked about a single account or post.

With a relay, requests to the directory go through it, encrypted so that only the directory can read them. The directory then does not see your internet address, and the relay does not see what you ask. Leave empty to ask the directory directly.

Pair this computer

For a computer without your app, such as a library computer. Likes you make here go, encrypted, to a mailbox; your app collects them and asks you before sending them. Nothing that can prove you are you is kept here. The pairing ends at the time you choose, or when you unpair. Closing the browser forgets the words here.
