# Belgium

## 1. Close the eID means

**eID card.** Call **DOC STOP, available 24/7: 0800 2123 2123, or 00322 518 2123** from abroad
([Police](https://www.police.be/shape/en/questions/lost-or-stolen/loss-or-theft-of-your-belgian-identity-document));
the free number 00800 2123 2123 works worldwide ([IBZ](https://ibz.be/en/node/2283)). The card and its
certificates are blocked at once. For 7 days the block can be lifted (if you find the card); after that
the card is cancelled for good ([Police](https://www.police.be/5283/contact/numeros-durgence/doc-stop)).

**itsme.** Block your account online at [my.prd.itsme.be/block](https://my.prd.itsme.be/block) with your
phone number and first name(s); you get an SMS confirmation ([itsme](https://www.itsme-id.com/help)).

## 2. Report to the police

If your identity card was stolen, make a statement to the police; a lost card is reported to the
Population department of your municipality
([Police](https://www.police.be/shape/en/questions/lost-or-stolen/loss-or-theft-of-your-belgian-identity-document),
[IBZ](https://ibz.be/en/node/2283)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new identity card at the municipality where you live ([IBZ](https://ibz.be/en/node/2283)).
itsme is reactivated in the app on your new phone ([itsme](https://www.itsme-id.com/help)).

## Not checked

An itsme phone line, online police reporting, and banks' own login means.
