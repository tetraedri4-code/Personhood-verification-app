# Hungary

## 1. Close the eID means

**Digital Citizenship app (DÁP).** If your phone is stolen or lost, make the app unusable remotely at
[dap.gov.hu](https://dap.gov.hu). You must register again afterwards. The government customer line is
**1818**, or **+36 1 550 1858** from abroad
([DÁP terms](https://app.dap.gov.hu/mobilapp-aszf/20240901.pdf)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse to the police.

## 3. Get new means

Register the DÁP app again ([DÁP terms](https://app.dap.gov.hu/mobilapp-aszf/20240901.pdf)).
