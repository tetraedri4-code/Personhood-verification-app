# Bulgaria

## 1. Close the eID means

**Evrotrust** is Bulgaria's notified eID scheme (a mobile app). Other means used for public services
include the personal identification codes (PIK) of the National Social Security Institute and of the
National Revenue Agency, and Borica ([Evrotrust](https://discover.evrotrust.com/eid-scheme-en),
[European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/display/EIDCOMMUNITY/Bulgaria)).
Contact the issuer of each means you use and ask them to block it.

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse and a lost or stolen ID card to the police (Ministry of Interior).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new ID card at the Ministry of Interior, then register your eID means again.

## Not checked

Any official blocking route: none was found for Evrotrust, the PIK codes or the ID card. Steps 2 and 3 are
general advice, not from a Bulgarian source.
