# Netherlands

## 1. Close the eID means

**DigiD.**

- If you can still log in, change your password at once in [Mijn DigiD](https://mijn.digid.nl). If the
  phone with the DigiD app was stolen, deactivate the app there ("Deze DigiD app deactiveren")
  ([DigiD](https://www.digid.nl/veiligheid/fraude-misbruik)).
- If you cannot, call the **DigiD helpdesk: 088 123 65 55, or +31 88 123 65 55 from abroad**. It is
  open on working days 8.00–22.00 and on Saturday 9.00–17.00, not around the clock
  ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/contact/contactgids/helpdesk-digid),
  [DigiD](https://www.digid.nl/veiligheid/fraude-misbruik)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report fraud at once and file a police report; victims of identity fraud also report to the Central
Reporting Point for Identity Fraud (CMI) ([DigiD](https://www.digid.nl/veiligheid/fraude-misbruik),
[CMI form](https://www.rvig.nl/centraal-meldpunt-identiteitsfraude/melding-doen-bij-het-cmi)). In most
cases the police report is made by appointment at a police station; online fraud can be reported online
at "Mijn Politie" ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/recht-veiligheid-en-defensie/identiteitsfraude/vraag-en-antwoord/identiteitsfraude/wat-doe-ik-bij-mogelijke-identiteitsfraude)).

## 3. Get new means

The DigiD app can be installed again on a new phone ([DigiD](https://www.digid.nl/veiligheid/fraude-misbruik)).

## Not checked

Blocking a lost identity card used for logging in, and closing a DigiD account altogether.
