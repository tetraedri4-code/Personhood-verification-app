# Luxembourg

## 1. Close the eID means

**eID card certificates (used with the GouvID app or a card reader).** Suspend or revoke them by phone
at **(+352) 24 550 550, available 24/7**, or in person at the Guichet.lu desk, 11 rue Notre-Dame,
L-2240 Luxembourg ([CTIE](https://ctie.gouvernement.lu/en/dossiers/eID/eID.html),
[Guichet.lu](https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/citoyennete/papiers-identite/carte-identite/perte-vol.html)).

**LuxTrust certificate.** Revoke it online; this cannot be undone and affects all devices of the
certificate. To stop only one device, suspend it under "Manage my devices" in MyLuxTrust. Without your
User ID or a device, call (+352) 24 550 550 ([LuxTrust](https://luxtrust.com/en/my-luxtrust/my-certificate/revoke)).

## 2. Report to the police

Declare the loss or theft online in MyGuichet.lu or at a police station; a theft report is passed to
the public prosecutor ([CTIE](https://ctie.gouvernement.lu/en/dossiers/eID/eID.html),
[Guichet.lu](https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/citoyennete/papiers-identite/carte-identite/perte-vol.html)).
Also block your payment cards with your bank, and your SIM card if your phone was stolen
([Guichet.lu](https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/life-event/citoyennete/agir-perte-document.html)).

## 3. Get new means

To use certificates again you need a new identity card, applied for at your commune or the Guichet.lu
desk with the police certificate of loss or theft ([CTIE](https://ctie.gouvernement.lu/en/dossiers/eID/eID.html),
[Guichet.lu](https://guichet.public.lu/en/citoyens/citoyennete/papiers-identite/carte-identite/perte-vol.html)).
A revoked LuxTrust product must be ordered again ([LuxTrust](https://luxtrust.com/en/my-luxtrust/my-certificate/revoke)).
