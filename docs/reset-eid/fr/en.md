# France

## 1. Close the eID means

**France Identité** (the state's digital identity app). Manage or delete it under "Gérer mon identité
numérique" at [idp.france-identite.gouv.fr](https://idp.france-identite.gouv.fr/suppression-identite/)
([France Identité](https://france-identite.gouv.fr/)).

**Identity card.** If it is lost, declare it at a police station or gendarmerie, unless you apply for a new
card straight away ([service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1344)).

Other login means used with public services, such as L'Identité Numérique La Poste, are closed through
their own provider ([service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1344)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse at a police station or gendarmerie.

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new identity card at a town hall (mairie) equipped for it, after an online pre-application
([service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1344)).
