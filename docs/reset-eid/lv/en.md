# Latvia

## 1. Close the eID means

**eID card.** Suspend the card's certificates by calling the LVRTC helpdesk. You are asked a secret
question. Do the same if you suspect someone else has your card. Suspended certificates are restored only
in person ([PMLP](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/if-your-personal-identification-document-passport-or-eid-card-has-been-lost-stolen-or-seized)).
The two official pages give different numbers: **67108787**
([PMLP, 2025](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/if-your-personal-identification-document-passport-or-eid-card-has-been-lost-stolen-or-seized))
and **+371 67018989** ([PMLP](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/what-do-case-loss-or-stealth-and-personal-certificate)).

**Smart-ID.** Delete the account in the [Smart-ID portal](https://portal.smart-id.com) or call customer
service **+371 6766 5001** ([Smart-ID](https://www.smart-id.com/help/customer-service/)).

## 2. Report to the police

Notify the Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP) and the State Police at once. Abroad, notify a
Latvian consular mission
([PMLP](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/if-your-personal-identification-document-passport-or-eid-card-has-been-lost-stolen-or-seized)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new document within 30 days
([PMLP](https://www.pmlp.gov.lv/en/if-your-personal-identification-document-passport-or-eid-card-has-been-lost-stolen-or-seized)).
