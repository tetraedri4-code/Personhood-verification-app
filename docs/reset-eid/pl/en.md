# Poland

## 1. Close the eID means

**Identity theft.** Report it at any municipal office (gmina), in person or online with the mObywatel
form (you need a trusted profile, e-ID card or qualified certificate; since 1 January 2026 the reply
comes through e-Doręczenia). The official voids your ID card at once. For adults, your PESEL number is then
restricted automatically
([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/zglos-nieuprawnione-wykorzystanie-swoich-danych-osobowych-kradziez-tozsamosci--uniewaznij-dowod)).

**ID card in the mObywatel app.** Under "Załatw sprawę" and then "Dowód osobisty", you can suspend a lost
ID card (reversible within 14 days) or void it
([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-kujawsko-pomorski/dokumenty-pod-kontrola---nowe-uslugi-w-mobywatelu)).

## 2. Report to the police

Notify the police as well; a scan of the police report can be attached to the online form
([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/uw-kujawsko-pomorski/dokumenty-pod-kontrola---nowe-uslugi-w-mobywatelu)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new ID card; it is free
([gov.pl](https://www.gov.pl/web/gov/zglos-nieuprawnione-wykorzystanie-swoich-danych-osobowych-kradziez-tozsamosci--uniewaznij-dowod)).
