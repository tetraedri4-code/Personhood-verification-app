# Estonia

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID card issued until 14 November 2025.** Suspend its certificates by calling **SK ID Solutions, 24/7,
+372 677 3377**, giving your name and personal identification code. Suspended certificates cannot be
reactivated ([id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/article/what-to-do-if-an-id-card-or-other-digital-document-is-lost-or-stolen-3/)).

**ID card issued from 17 November 2025.** Its certificates cannot be suspended, only revoked: in the
Police and Border Guard Board's self-service at
[revocation-portal.eidpki.ee](https://revocation-portal.eidpki.ee/en/landing) or at a service centre
([id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/article/what-to-do-if-an-id-card-or-other-digital-document-is-lost-or-stolen-3/)).

**Mobile-ID.** Call your mobile operator to revoke the certificates: Elisa +372 6600 600, Telia
+372 6397 130, Tele2 +372 686 6866 ([id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/article/suspending-mobile-id-certificates/)).
You can see your active tools in the [myID portal](https://myid.skidsolutions.eu/en).

**Smart-ID.** Delete the account in the [Smart-ID portal](https://portal.smart-id.com), at a bank office, or
by calling customer service **+372 715 1606** ([Smart-ID](https://www.smart-id.com/help/faq/closing-the-account/phone-got-stolen-lost/),
[id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/id-card/)).

## 2. Report to the police

If the card was stolen, close the certificates, revoke the card and make a statement to the police
([id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/article/what-to-do-if-an-id-card-or-other-digital-document-is-lost-or-stolen-3/)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new ID card at a service point or in the self-service
([id.ee](https://www.id.ee/en/article/what-to-do-if-an-id-card-or-other-digital-document-is-lost-or-stolen-3/)).
