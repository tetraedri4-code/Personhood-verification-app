# Austria

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID Austria** (which replaced the mobile phone signature, Handy-Signatur). According to the ID Austria
whitepaper for users, if your login data are compromised you revoke the signature certificate, and if you
lose your phone you log out all devices or reset the app
([whitepaper, 2021](https://www.id-austria.gv.at/dam/jcr:1ee37fa4-5781-4db3-9839-b12417bd917f/ID-Austria-Whitepaper-fuer-EndanwenderInnen.docx.pdf)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse to the police.

## 3. Get new means

Register a new ID Austria at a registration authority
([Federal Chancellery](https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/agenda/digitalisierung/digitales-oesterreich/id-austria.html)),
for example a passport authority, an authorised municipality or a tax office
([oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/dam/jcr:0c7d8ab4-c806-4138-94e3-b2f8a501f3c5/220923_Folder_ID_Austria_Web.pdf)).

## Not checked

The current revocation steps and any hotline (the 2021 whitepaper was read only in summary), and the
police's online reporting. Step 2 is general advice, not from an Austrian source.
