# Lithuania

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID card.** Report the loss online in the MIGRIS system, at any time of day, or in person; not by post,
phone or e-mail. Abroad, report it at an embassy
([MFA](https://uk.mfa.lt/en/consular-services/consular-issues/lost-your-passport-or-identity-card/231)).

**Smart-ID.** Delete the account in the [Smart-ID portal](https://portal.smart-id.com) or call customer
service **+370 67041807** ([Smart-ID](https://www.smart-id.com/help/customer-service/)).

## 2. Report to the police

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs recommends reporting a loss or theft to the local police
([MFA](https://uk.mfa.lt/en/consular-services/consular-issues/lost-your-passport-or-identity-card/231)).
The police have an online portal, [ePolicija](https://www.epolicija.lt/).

## 3. Get new means

Book a time for a new document through e-registration
([MFA](https://uk.mfa.lt/en/consular-services/consular-issues/lost-your-passport-or-identity-card/231)).

## Not checked

A hotline for suspending ID card certificates, the mobile signature, and banks' own login means.
