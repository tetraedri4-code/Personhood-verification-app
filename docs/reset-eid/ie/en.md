# Ireland

## 1. Close the eID means

**Public Services Card (used for MyGovID).** If it is lost or stolen, immediately contact the Public
Service Helpdesk ([gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/fd52ab-public-services-card)).

**MyGovID account.** Change your password and turn on multi-factor authentication, as the National Cyber
Security Centre advises for any compromised account ([NCSC](https://sme.ncsc.gov.ie/account-compromise)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report it to your local Garda station ([NCSC](https://sme.ncsc.gov.ie/account-compromise)).

## 3. Get new means

A new Public Services Card needs a SAFE Registration appointment
([gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/fd52ab-public-services-card)). The MyGovID app needs a valid
Irish passport book ([gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/publication/f61b1-mygovid-app)).

## Not checked

The Public Service Helpdesk's phone number, whether MyGovID can be blocked by phone, and online Garda
reporting.
