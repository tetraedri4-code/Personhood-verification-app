# Sweden

## 1. Close the eID means

**BankID.** BankID is blocked through the bank that issued it. Block it at once, by phone or at a branch.
For example, Swedbank's blocking line **08 411 10 11** is open around the clock
([Swedbank](https://www.swedbank.se/om-oss/sakerhet-och-bedragerier/sparrhjalp)). Use your own bank's
blocking number.

**Freja eID.** Call the blocking line **+46 8-27 00 29**, open 24/7, only for urgent blocking
([Freja](https://frejaeid.com/freja-support/)).

**ID card or passport.** If it is lost or stolen, call the police on **114 14** or visit a police station
([Police](https://polisen.se/tjanster-tillstand/pass-och-nationellt-id-kort/)).

Never use your eID at the request of someone who calls you
([Skatteverket](https://skatteverket.se/servicelankar/otherlanguages/englishengelska/news/news2025/news/threewaystoreducetheriskofidentitytheft.5.6491ed82199c4722dae51e.html)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report other crimes, such as misuse of your identity, through the police e-services or on 114 14
([Police](https://polisen.se/utsatt-for-brott/polisanmalan/)).

## 3. Get new means

Order a new BankID from your bank. Book a time with the police for a new ID card or passport
([Police](https://polisen.se/tjanster-tillstand/pass-och-nationellt-id-kort/)).

## Not checked

Blocking numbers of banks other than Swedbank.
