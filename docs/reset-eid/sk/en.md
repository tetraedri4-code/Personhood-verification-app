# Slovakia

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID card with chip (eID).** Report the loss or theft at once at any police unit, or abroad at a Slovak
embassy. The card becomes invalid when reported. Ask the district police directorate to revoke all
certificates; bring the certificate contract and the card number
([slovensko.sk](https://www.slovensko.sk/sk/zivotne-situacie/zivotna-situacia/_ukradli-mi-doklady-stratili)).

You can also report it online with the service "Nahlásenie straty alebo odcudzenia občianskeho preukazu
s čipom" on slovensko.sk. It needs a confidential item you set earlier with the service "Modifikácia
kontaktných údajov"; the certificates are then revoked automatically
([slovensko.sk](https://www.slovensko.sk/sk/zivotne-situacie/zivotna-situacia/_ukradli-mi-doklady-stratili)).

## 2. Report to the police

The report above is made to the police; you get a confirmation that serves as a temporary document
([slovensko.sk](https://www.slovensko.sk/sk/zivotne-situacie/zivotna-situacia/_ukradli-mi-doklady-stratili)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new card within 30 days at any district police directorate or Slovak embassy; it costs €25
([slovensko.sk](https://www.slovensko.sk/sk/zivotne-situacie/zivotna-situacia/_ukradli-mi-doklady-stratili)).
