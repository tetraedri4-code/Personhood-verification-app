# Spain

## 1. Close the eID means

**Cl@ve Firma certificate.** Revoke it online (Electronic Identification, Cl@ve Móvil, All services, Other
services, Revoke) or in the Cl@ve app (Transactions, Cl@ve Signature, Revoke). You need your DNI or NIE and
the document's serial number. The same page links to a form to cancel Cl@ve altogether
([Agencia Tributaria](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/en_gb/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/firma-digital-sistema-clave-pin-tecnica/modificar-datos-renunciar-servicio-clave/revocar-certificado-clave-firma.html)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse to the Policía Nacional or Guardia Civil.

## 3. Get new means

Register for Cl@ve again once your documents are secured.

## Not checked

Blocking the DNIe's certificates, a phone line, and online police reports. Steps 2 and 3 are general
advice, not from a Spanish source.
