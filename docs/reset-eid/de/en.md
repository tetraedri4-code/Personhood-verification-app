# Germany

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID card online function (Online-Ausweisfunktion, eID).** Since 2017 every ID card is issued with the
online function switched on ([FIM](https://fimportal.de/api/v0/xzufi-services/S100002/S1000020010000012152/de/pdf)).

- **Phone, around the clock: the blocking hotline 116 116.** It is always reachable and free from within
  Germany; calls from mobiles or from abroad may cost ([FIM](https://fimportal.de/api/v0/xzufi-services/S100002/S1000020010000012152/de/pdf),
  [Munich](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-oder-diebstahl-personalausweis/1076889/)).
- You need your **blocking password (Sperrkennwort)** from the PIN letter. Without it, block in person
  at the authority that issued the card, with proof of identity ([FIM](https://fimportal.de/api/v0/xzufi-services/S100002/S1000020010000012152/de/pdf)).
- Reporting the loss at the citizens' office (Bürgerbüro) or to the police also blocks the online
  function ([Mainz](https://internet.mainz.de/en/vv/produkte/buergeramt/personalausweis-verlust-anzeigen)).
  Many offices need an appointment ([Munich](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-oder-diebstahl-personalausweis/1076889/)).

**Qualified electronic signature (QES) on the card.** It is blocked separately, by the provider you
bought the signature certificate from ([Munich](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-oder-diebstahl-personalausweis/1076889/),
[Bremen](https://www.service.bremen.de/dienstleistungen/revocation-of-signature-certificates-for-electronic-proof-of-identity-164983)).

## 2. Report to the police

The City of Munich recommends reporting a loss or theft to the police
([Munich](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-oder-diebstahl-personalausweis/1076889/)).
A loss reported at the citizens' office is passed on to the police
([Mainz](https://internet.mainz.de/en/vv/produkte/buergeramt/personalausweis-verlust-anzeigen)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new ID card at the citizens' office, which can be done when you report the loss; it takes
four to five weeks, and a temporary ID is possible in urgent cases. A blocked online function is
switched on again only in person ([Munich](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/verlust-oder-diebstahl-personalausweis/1076889/),
[Mainz](https://internet.mainz.de/en/vv/produkte/buergeramt/personalausweis-verlust-anzeigen)).

## Not checked

The BundID account, the police forces' online reporting (Onlinewachen of the federal states), and
procedures of cities other than Munich, Mainz, Bremen and Hamburg.
