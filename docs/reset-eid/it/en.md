# Italy

## 1. Close the eID means

**SPID.** Each SPID identity is blocked by its own provider. A suspension lasts up to 30 days; the
emergency block in the provider's self-service is a suspension; a revocation cannot be undone
([AgID user guide](https://www.agid.gov.it/sites/default/files/repository_files/guida-utente_spid_v4.pdf)).
For example, LepidaID suspends by phone on **800.779077** or by certified e-mail (PEC) to
lepidaid@pec.lepida.it; revocation after loss or theft needs the complaint to the authorities attached
([LepidaID form](https://id.lepida.it/docs/modulo_revoca_sospensione.pdf)). Use your own provider's
channels.

**Electronic identity card (CIE).** Call **800 098558** to revoke it
([CIE portal](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/)).

## 2. Report to the police

File a complaint (denuncia) with the police or Carabinieri; SPID providers ask for it to revoke an identity
([LepidaID form](https://id.lepida.it/docs/modulo_revoca_sospensione.pdf)).

## 3. Get new means

Request a new CIE at any municipality (comune)
([CIE portal](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/)), and a new SPID from a provider.

## Not checked

Blocking channels of SPID providers other than LepidaID and InfoCamere, and the hours of the numbers
above.
