# Portugal

## 1. Close the eID means

**Citizen card (Cartão de Cidadão).** If it is lost, stolen or taken, cancel it. Cancel it online in the
gov.pt app or on the gov.pt portal, by phone on the **Citizen Card line (+351) 210 990 111, open 24 hours a
day, 7 days a week**, or at a service desk
([gov.pt guide](https://www.gov.pt/guias/comunicar-e-cancelar-os-cartoes-perdidos-ou-roubados)). You have
10 days from learning what happened to cancel it, and a cancelled card cannot be reactivated
([gov.pt](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/cancelar-o-cartao-de-cidadao)).

**Digital Mobile Key (Chave Móvel Digital).** Only the holder can cancel it, at any time; it becomes
inactive at once ([gov.pt](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/cancelar-a-chave-movel-digital)). Cancel it as
well: the key stays active until 30 days after the expiry date of a cancelled card
([gov.pt](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/cancelar-o-cartao-de-cidadao)).

## 2. Report to the police

Go to a police station (PSP) or GNR post as soon as possible and keep the report. You can also tell the
Banco de Portugal that your identity documents were lost or stolen, with its
[online form](https://clientebancario.bportugal.pt/formulario-extravio), attaching the police report
([gov.pt guide](https://www.gov.pt/guias/comunicar-e-cancelar-os-cartoes-perdidos-ou-roubados)).

## 3. Get new means

Request a new citizen card through the renewal service, and activate a new Digital Mobile Key
([gov.pt](https://www.gov.pt/servicos/cancelar-o-cartao-de-cidadao)).
