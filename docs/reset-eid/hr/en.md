# Croatia

## 1. Close the eID means

**eOI (ID card with chip).** Report a loss or suspected abuse at once at the nearest police station; the
card's certificates are then suspended automatically. You can also report it online in the My eID portal,
or abroad at a consulate ([eid.hr](https://eid.hr/en/electronic-id/articles/lost-or-stolen-eid)).

**Other credentials** used for e-Citizens (for example Certilia, bank tokens, FINA mToken) are blocked by
their issuer; the list is on [gov.hr](https://gov.hr/hr/lista-prihvacenih-vjerodajnica/1792?lang=en).

## 2. Report to the police

The report above is made to the police ([eid.hr](https://eid.hr/en/electronic-id/articles/lost-or-stolen-eid)).

## 3. Get new means

Only the Ministry of the Interior issues the card's credentials; get a new PIN at a police station
([eid.hr FAQ](https://eid.hr/en/faq)).

## Not checked

The issuers' blocking numbers.
