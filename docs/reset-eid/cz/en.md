# Czechia

## 1. Close the eID means

**Login means in Identita občana.** Log in at
[identita.gov.cz, login means](https://www.identita.gov.cz/obcan/prihlasovaci-prostredky), choose a means
and click "Zamknout" (lock). You can lock any means, including the one you are logged in with; "Okamžitě
uzamknout" locks it at once. You need at least one verified contact (e-mail or phone) under "Moje údaje".
The lock is temporary, and it stops only logins to state services: a bank identity keeps working in the
private sector ([Lupa.cz, 2025](https://www.lupa.cz/clanky/jak-funguje-zamykani-prihlasovacich-prostredku-v-identite-obcana/)).
So also contact your bank if you log in with a bank identity.

**ID card.** Report a loss, theft or misuse without delay at a municipal office with extended powers (in
Prague, any district office Prague 1 to 22) or at a registry office, by data box, by e-mail with a
recognised electronic signature, or with the Ministry of the Interior's
[online form](https://dfmv-p.gov.cz/web/form?type=F_OP_ZTRATA&version=latest). The card stops being valid
once reported; reporting is free ([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/ohlaseni-ztraty-nebo-odcizeni-obcanskeho-prukazu-S186)).

## 2. Report to the police

A theft can also be reported to the Police of the Czech Republic
([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/ohlaseni-ztraty-nebo-odcizeni-obcanskeho-prukazu-S186)).

## 3. Get new means

A new ID card costs CZK 200
([portal.gov.cz](https://portal.gov.cz/en/sluzby-vs/ohlaseni-ztraty-nebo-odcizeni-obcanskeho-prukazu-S186)).
