# Finland

## 1. Close the eID means

**The law.** The holder of an identification means must tell the provider without undue delay when it
is lost, in someone else's possession or misused. The provider must make it possible to report this at
any time, and must then revoke or block the means without delay ([Act 617/2009 § 25](https://finlex.fi/api/media/statute/69585/mainPdf/main.pdf)).

**Bank codes and bank apps.** At Nordea they cannot be closed from the online bank; call
([Nordea](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/sinun-elamasi/turvallisuus/jouduitko-huijatuksi.html)).

| Bank | What to do | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Nordea | Call customer service and choose the fraud line (key 1). When it is closed, call the blocking service, open around the clock ([Nordea](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/sinun-elamasi/turvallisuus/jouduitko-huijatuksi.html), [Nordea credentials](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/palvelumme/verkko-mobiilipalvelut/pankkitunnukset/)) | 0200 3000; blocking service 020 333 (+358 20 333 from abroad) |
| OP | Call customer service (weekdays 8–16). When it is closed, call the blocking service, open around the clock, and have the OP codes and card closed ([OP](https://www.op.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/asiakkuus/turvallisuus)) | 0100 0500; blocking service 0100 0555 |
| Other banks | The law above applies to every bank. Look for "sulkupalvelu" on the bank's own pages | |

**Mobile certificate (mobiilivarmenne).**

| Operator | What to do | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Elisa | Report the loss of the phone or PIN, or that someone else knows the PIN, without delay. The certificate closes when the subscription is blocked: block it yourself in OmaElisa ("Liittymän tilapäinen sulku"), or call; lost devices are taken around the clock ([Elisa FAQ](https://elisa.fi/asiakaspalvelu/aihe/mobiilivarmenne/ohje/usein-kysyttya/), [Elisa lost SIM](https://elisa.fi/asiakaspalvelu/puheliittymat/sim-tai-laite-kadonnut/)) | 010 190 240 |
| Telia | Blocking the subscription or the Mobile ID is handled as an emergency, around the clock ([Telia](https://www.telia.fi/asiakastuki/palvelut/mobile-certificate)) | 020 690 400 |

A mobile certificate can be registered again with online bank codes ([Telia](https://www.telia.fi/asiakastuki/palvelut/mobile-certificate)).
If your bank codes may also be known to someone, close those too.

**ID card certificate (kansalaisvarmenne).** If the card is lost or stolen, inform DVV's revocation
service and the police; a new card is ordered from the police ([DVV](https://dvv.fi/en/citizen-certificate)).
The revocation service's number is on that page.

## 2. Report to the police

Identity theft is reported to the police as a crime; Nordea says the same of misused bank codes
([Nordea credentials](https://www.nordea.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/palvelumme/verkko-mobiilipalvelut/pankkitunnukset/)).
The report can be made online in the police e-services (asiointi.poliisi.fi)
([Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/omatieto/fi/ukk/jos-ep%E4ilen-identiteettivarkautta%2C-niin-miss%E4-kohtaa-minun-kannattaa-tehd%E4-rikosilmoitus)).
Asiakastieto also advises contacting your bank, changing passwords at once and considering a credit
ban ([Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/omatieto/fi/ukk/jos-havaitsen-identiteettivarkauden-mita-minun-tulee-tehda-aivan-ensimmaisena)).

## 3. Get new means

New bank codes come from your bank and a new mobile certificate from your operator; a new ID card is
ordered from the police ([DVV](https://dvv.fi/en/citizen-certificate)).

## Not checked

The police's own pages (they refused automated reading), DNA and other operators, banks other than
Nordea and OP, and the number of DVV's revocation service.
