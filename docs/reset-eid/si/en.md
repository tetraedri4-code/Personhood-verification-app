# Slovenia

## 1. Close the eID means

**ID card.** Report a lost ID card to any administrative unit within 8 days; reporting only to the police
is not enough. Abroad, report it to a consulate within 30 days
([e-uprava](https://e-uprava.gov.si/en/podrocja/personal-documents-certificates-change-of-residence/personal-documents-digital-certificate/izguba-pogresitev-osebne-izkazice.html)).

**SI-PASS and smsPASS.** Cancel smsPASS on your SI-PASS user pages
([SI-TRUST](https://www.si-trust.gov.si/en/si-pass/mobile-identity-smspass/)). Support: **ekc@gov.si,
+386 1 478 8590**; smsPASS **+386 080 2002**
([SI-TRUST terms](https://www.si-trust.gov.si/en/si-pass-smspass-general-terms)).

## 2. Report to the police

Report identity misuse to the police as well; for the card, the administrative unit is required
([e-uprava](https://e-uprava.gov.si/en/podrocja/personal-documents-certificates-change-of-residence/personal-documents-digital-certificate/izguba-pogresitev-osebne-izkazice.html)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new ID card at once
([e-uprava](https://e-uprava.gov.si/en/podrocja/personal-documents-certificates-change-of-residence/personal-documents-digital-certificate/izguba-pogresitev-osebne-izkazice.html)).

## Not checked

The hours of the support lines, and revoking qualified certificates (SIGEN-CA and others).
