# Denmark

## 1. Close the eID means

**MitID.** If you suspect misuse, block your MitID at once. A block stops all use of it
([MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/hjaelpeunivers/mistanke-om-misbrug/)). There are five ways to block
the MitID app, and uninstalling the app is not enough
([MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/hjaelpeunivers/mitid-app/spaer-mitid-app/)):

- on MitID.dk **without logging in**: [mitid.dk/self-service/authenticators-revocation](https://www.mitid.dk/self-service/authenticators-revocation/);
- on MitID.dk when logged in, or in the app;
- by phone: **MitID Support +45 33 98 00 10**, Monday to Friday 8 to 20, weekends and holidays 10 to 16
  ([borger.dk](https://www.borger.dk/hjaelp-og-vejledning/hvad-har-du-brug-for-hjaelp-til/mitid));
- at Borgerservice in your municipality (book a time, bring a passport or driving licence).

Afterwards, look at the recent activity on your MitID profile page
([MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/hjaelpeunivers/spaerring/)).

## 2. Report to the police

MitID says to always report misuse to the police
([MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/hjaelpeunivers/mistanke-om-misbrug/)). Identity theft and fraud are
reported on the police website:
[politi.dk, report online fraud](https://politi.dk/oekonomisk-svindel-paa-nettet/anmeld-oekonomisk-svindel-paa-nettet).
The non-urgent police number is 114 ([Police](https://politi.dk/)).

## 3. Get new means

Reactivate MitID or get a new app or code display through MitID.dk or Borgerservice
([MitID](https://www.mitid.dk/hjaelp/hjaelpeunivers/spaerring/),
[borger.dk](https://www.borger.dk/hjaelp-og-vejledning/hvad-har-du-brug-for-hjaelp-til/mitid)).

## Not checked

Whether a 24-hour blocking line exists outside MitID Support's opening hours, and banks' own procedures.
