# Malta

## 1. Close the eID means

**eID card.** File a report of the lost or stolen card at your local police station or at the Identity
Cards Unit; this is free. Identità's phone number is **+356 2590 4000**
([Identità](https://identita.gov.mt/identity-cards-unit/change-in-details/lost-or-stolen-eid-card/)).

## 2. Report to the police

The report above is made at a police station or at the Identity Cards Unit
([Identità](https://identita.gov.mt/identity-cards-unit/change-in-details/lost-or-stolen-eid-card/)).

## 3. Get new means

Apply for a new card with Form ID10a (€22). Form ID9 gives a temporary document
([Identità](https://identita.gov.mt/identity-cards-unit/change-in-details/lost-or-stolen-eid-card/)).
