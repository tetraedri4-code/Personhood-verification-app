# Romania

## 1. Close the eID means

**Electronic identity card (CEI).** Its certificates are used to log in to public authorities
([DJEP Iași](https://www.djep-iasi.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GENERALITATI-CEI.pdf)). According to a
consumer association, declare a loss at the counter, usually within 15 days, and tell your bank
([InfoCons](https://infocons.ro/ai-ramas-fara-buletin-afla-care-sunt-pasii-de-urmat-si-cum-poti-obtine-noua-carte-electronica-de-identitate-cei-infocons-protectia-consumatorilor-te-informeaza/)).

**ROeID** is the single sign-on for public services ([ROeID](https://www.roeid.ro/)).

## 2. Report to the police

Tell the police if you suspect theft
([InfoCons](https://infocons.ro/ai-ramas-fara-buletin-afla-care-sunt-pasii-de-urmat-si-cum-poti-obtine-noua-carte-electronica-de-identitate-cei-infocons-protectia-consumatorilor-te-informeaza/)).

## 3. Get new means

Book an appointment for a new CEI on the [Ministry of Internal Affairs hub](https://hub.mai.gov.ro/); a
repeat card costs 70 lei ([DJEP Iași](https://www.djep-iasi.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GENERALITATI-CEI.pdf)).
